# Causality-Assessment-Task-
Patient: 68-year-old male, Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (diagnosed 8 years ago), mild chronic kidney disease (eGFR 48 mL/min/1.73m²) 

Drug history: Metformin 500 mg BID, started 3 years ago; dose increased to 1000 mg BID six weeks prior to presentation due to poor glycemic control

Day 0: Dose increased to 1000 mg BID

Day 10: Patient develops nausea, vomiting, and abdominal discomfort

Day 14: Presents to ED with lethargy, tachypnea, hypotension

Labs: Serum lactate 6.8mmol/L (normal <2.0), arterial pH 7.21, anion gap elevated at 22, serum creatinine risen from 1.3 to 2.1 mg/dL
Metformin was withheld; patient managed with IV fluids and bicarbonate

Day 17: Lactate normalized to 1.4 mmol/L, symptoms resolved, renal function trending back toward baseline

Metformin was not rechallenged given the diagnosis and residual renal impairment

Naranjo Scale Scoring
Total Score: 6 → Probable
